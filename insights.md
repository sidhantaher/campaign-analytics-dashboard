# Insights: Bank Campaign Performance

Overall conversion: 11.27% (4,639 of 41,176 contacts)

## Findings
1. Channel: cellular converts at 14.74% vs 5.23% for telephone (about 2.8x).
2. Prior outcome: previous success converts at 65.11% vs 8.83% for new contacts. Previous failures convert at 14.23%.
3. Timing and effort: May has the most contacts (13,767) but the lowest conversion (6.44%). Mar, Sep, Oct and Dec convert at 44-51% on small volumes. Conversion drops from 13.04% on the first attempt to 4.61% at 7+ attempts.

## Recommendations
1. Shift outreach from telephone to cellular.
2. Prioritize past converters and re-engage past failures before cold contacts.
3. Cap follow-ups at about 3 attempts and move some May volume into high-converting months.

## Caveats
- Mar and Dec have small samples (546 and 182 contacts), so treat them as signals, not proof.
- Students (31%) and retirees (25%) convert best but are small segments.
- This is bank data, not WhatsApp data. The method transfers; the numbers do not.
